# Privacy Policy - Eastfield: Airport Architect

Last updated: 01/11/2026

This policy explains what "Eastfield: Airport Architect" (the game) does with information. The game is made by qafi ("we"). Contact: kontak.mkd@gmail.com.

## What the game stores on your device
The game saves your airport, your settings (volume, music, full screen, autosave, tutorial) and small preferences on your own device. This data stays on your device. We do not receive it, and the game has no accounts, no sign-in and no online features of its own.

## Information we collect
We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information ourselves.

## Advertising (only in versions that show ads)
Some versions of the game on Android and iOS show optional rewarded video ads and a small banner ad using Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). If ads are turned on, Unity may collect and use device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device type, operating system and ad interaction data, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. This is handled by Unity under its own privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

You can limit ad personalisation in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking). Versions of the game without ads (for example browser and PC versions) do not use advertising services.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given information through an ad service, contact us and we will help.

## Sharing
We do not sell your information. Information is only handled by the ad service described above, if ads are on.

## Deleting your data
Uninstall the game to remove everything it stored on your device. For advertising data held by Unity, use the controls described in Unity's privacy policy.

## Changes
We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
